# Apexatix Pro Bot

## Market data providers

All requested asset candles use Twelve Data as the primary feed and Finnhub as the only fallback. When Twelve Data succeeds and `FINNHUB_API_KEY` is configured, Finnhub is queried as an independent comparison source. If Twelve Data fails, Finnhub may serve the candles, and the comparison status is marked unavailable because there is no second successful source. No Binance, Coinbase, OANDA, Yahoo Finance, or Gold API market-data requests are made. Provider-specific symbol coverage and plan limits still apply; if neither configured feed returns usable candles, the API reports both errors and no signal is produced.

Configure `TWELVE_DATA_API_KEY` and `FINNHUB_API_KEY` as server-side environment variables for local development and deployment. Do not expose either key in browser code.

## Market scanning and alerts

The dashboard defaults to XAU/USD, BTC/USD, EUR/USD, and AUD/USD; silver is not in the default watchlist, but users can add assets manually. It refreshes closed-candle market data while open and auto-scan is enabled. The default strategy and scheduled scanner follow market direction: EMA9/EMA21, MACD, and RSI above/below 50 must agree on the 45-minute bullish/bearish regime; the entry timeframe must agree, and at least two of the 15m, 30m, and 45m trends must align. This produces BUY setups only in a confirmed bullish trend and SELL setups only in a confirmed bearish trend. The optional **Trend + Dual-TF RSI Confluence** strategy adds a pullback requirement: the 45m trend and selected entry-timeframe EMA/MACD must agree, then 15m and 30m RSI must both confirm an oversold pullback (≤30 and ≤38) for BUY or overbought pullback (≥70 and ≥62) for SELL.

The **Conservative Day-Trade Pullback** option applies stricter entry gates: both feeds must verify the same completed candle, volume data must be available and not unusually low, the 30m and 45m EMA/MACD/RSI trends must agree, the selected entry timeframe's EMA/MACD must confirm that direction, and 15m RSI must reset to 40–<50 for BUY or >50–60 for SELL. The general Dual-TF RSI, EMA/MACD, and Bollinger/RSI strategies also require the signal direction to agree with the confirmed 45m trend, preventing counter-trend BUYs in bearish regimes and counter-trend SELLs in bullish regimes. A bearish 45m trend can produce SELL setups when the selected strategy's own entry conditions confirm; if any required trend or entry signal is missing or conflicts, the result is an explanatory HOLD. BUY/SELL signals use the existing ATR-based invalidation reference (normally 1.5× ATR, refined by nearby support/resistance where justified) and a 2:1 target; this does not calculate position size or guarantee limited losses. Separately, the GitHub Actions workflow calls `/api/bot` every 15 minutes; when `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL` is configured, that scheduled scan sends the default trend-following advisory BUY/SELL/HOLD reports to Discord. Set the repository secrets `VERCEL_URL` and `CRON_SECRET` for the scheduled workflow. A BUY/SELL alert includes a rule-based confidence tier, the strategy rationale, an ISO timestamp, provider and candle-close time, RSI/EMA/MACD/ATR snapshot, and ATR-based invalidation and 2:1 target levels. HOLD decisions include their reason and market snapshot.

When both feeds return candles, a same-candle price disagreement above 0.5% for traditional markets or 1% for crypto suppresses entries. Source verification and provider details continue to inform signal validation but are omitted from dashboard cards and summaries.

Signals are held during quiet volatility (ATR below 0.02% of price), an erratic candle (range above 3 times the recent median), or available volume below half its recent average. Volume feeds are not universal; when volume is unavailable, confidence is capped rather than treating missing volume as adequate. These diagnostics influence decisions but are omitted from dashboard cards. Regimes are classified as Trending, Ranging, Volatile, or Quiet. The default trend-following strategy uses a 2-of-3 timeframe alignment threshold; the optional RSI, EMA/MACD, and Bollinger/RSI strategies retain their adaptive weighted composite score of at least 0.45. Confidence reflects cross-timeframe alignment and data quality.

Stops start at 1.5 ATR and may be widened just beyond nearby recent support/resistance; targets are recalculated at 2R. Recent closed candles are also used for simplified directional backtests, with trade count, win rate, and net R calculated internally. The trend-following historical sample uses the EMA/MACD/RSI direction rule; live entries additionally require the multi-timeframe alignment above. Backtests use a maximum 12-candle holding window, treat same-candle stop/target touches conservatively as stop-outs, and exclude fees, spread, and slippage. They are informational only: thresholds remain fixed and are not auto-tuned. Diagnostic inputs such as regime, ATR, volume availability, feed verification, support/resistance reasoning, and backtest metrics inform filtering and confidence but are not displayed in the dashboard cards.

Confidence is a confluence label, not a probability or a guarantee. Browser notifications are optional and require enabling the trade-alert control in the dashboard. Dashboard scans pause when the page is closed or auto-scan is disabled; the separately configured GitHub Actions scan continues on its 15-minute schedule. Alerts are advisory: the bot does not execute orders or calculate position size. The user must confirm executable broker prices, instrument contract specifications, and account-specific risk limits before acting.

Routine HOLD results are grouped into a single summary per manual scan rather than one log entry per asset. Auto-scan does not repeatedly log unchanged no-setup outcomes; individual HOLD reasons remain visible on the asset cards, while actionable signals and data-quality warnings remain in the transparency log.
